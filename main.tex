\title{Causal Probability Substrate Gravity: A Unified, Computational Model of Emergent Spacetime, Collapse, and Gravity}

\author{Justin Marks}
\affiliation{Independent Researcher, Long Island, NY, USA}

\date{\today}

\begin{abstract}
We propose a discrete, background-free model in which spacetime geometry, objective collapse, and gravity emerge from a single local scalar defined on a quantum causal graph. Each tick applies unitary evolution, state-dependent collapse, and thermodynamically budgeted graph rewiring. We define a Lorentzian-consistent curvature proxy from causal-cone volume and show, across seeds, sizes, and parameter sweeps, a robust negative correlation between curvature and mass charge, coincident with structure formation. Ablations establish necessity of both collapse and rewiring. All results are reproducible via public notebooks and a Dockerized pipeline.
\end{abstract}
